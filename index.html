About the chapter

Medical Laboratory Science, explained — and lived.

Medical Laboratory Science is the discipline behind almost every diagnosis. Every blood test, every scan that gets confirmed, every disease that gets caught early — a medical laboratory scientist ran that bench. It's the profession working quietly behind the doctor's confident answer.

NIMELSSA is the Nigerian Medical Laboratory Science Students' Association — the national body for MLS students across the country. The KDU chapter is our home at Kola Daisi University: the association that turns a course of study into a community, with our own communities, competitions, and traditions built by students, for students.

Code of Conduct

As NIMELSSA operates both as an academic student body and the training ground for future Medical Laboratory Scientists, members follow a dual code of conduct: the General Association Rules set out in the NIMELSSA Constitution, and the professional Medical Laboratory Science Code of Conduct.

General Association Rules

  • Uphold supremacy — respect the association's constitution as the highest guiding principle.
  • Maintain unity — foster peace and cooperation among all medical laboratory students.
  • Perform duties — fulfil financial dues, attend congresses, and participate in official student assignments.
  • Protect integrity — engage only in behaviours that protect the image and resources of the association.

Professional & Laboratory Codes of Conduct

  • Patient welfare first — place the health and well-being of the patient above personal interests.
  • Strict confidentiality — never disclose patient results or case details to unauthorized individuals.
  • Complete honesty — deliver and report diagnostic test results with absolute scientific precision and truthfulness.
  • Zero extortion — reject bribes, personal gratifications, or unapproved gifts from patients and relatives.
  • No personal gain — do not misuse specialized laboratory knowledge or skills for illicit self-enrichment.
  • Workplace integrity — never remove tools, reagents, or property belonging to the laboratory facility.
  • Professional sobriety — do not consume alcohol or unprescribed drugs that impair performance during working hours.
  • Interprofessional respect — work in absolute harmony with fellow students, scientists, and healthcare team members.
